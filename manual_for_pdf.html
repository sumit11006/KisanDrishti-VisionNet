🤝 How Each Module Helps the Other:

  • Satellite helps Diagnosis: Satellite multi-spectral data (NDVI/EVI) and micro-climate sensors detect elevated spore incubation conditions before symptoms fully erupt, putting the diagnosis engine on alert.
  • Diagnosis helps Voice: A rural farmer might not be able to read English scientific names like Phytophthora infestans. The diagnosis output feeds directly into the voice engine, speaking the dosage in their native language (e.g., Hindi: "2.5 ग्राम मैंकोजेब प्रति लीटर पानी").
  • Diagnosis helps BRICS Exchange: Local farm diagnoses do not stay trapped on the phone. Once confirmed, an anonymized observation record is packaged into JSON-LD and shared across BRICS agricultural research institutions (ICAR India, EMBRAPA Brazil, ARC South Africa) to track global pathogen spread.
  • Satellite helps Soil & Carbon: Time-series NDVI monitoring verifies that the farmer practiced cover-cropping (Sesbania/Mustard) between harvest seasons, calculating verified carbon sequestration (1,850 kg CO₂e/ha/yr) for green carbon credits.

🖥️ PART 2: Page-by-Page Deep Dive — Functionality, Technology & Buttons

PAGE 1: 🔬 Crop Diagnosis (Custom MobileNetV3 CNN + Grad-CAM)

1. Purpose:
Instant, offline, edge-native crop disease diagnosis from leaf images, providing explainable AI heatmaps, treatment schedules, and dynamic financial loss projections.

2. Technologies Used:

  • PyTorch 2.x & Torchvision: Core deep learning engine.
  • Custom MobileNetV3-Small: Lightweight convolutional architecture.
  • Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping): Autograd backward hooks on the last convolutional layer.
  • Pillow (PIL) & NumPy: Image decoding, resizing, matrix transformations, and colormap blending.
  • FastAPI (/api/v1/diagnose): Async backend receiving multipart form-data.
  • Tailwind CSS & Vanilla JavaScript: Dynamic client-side DOM manipulation without heavy frameworks.

3. Every Button & Control Explained:

  • Tomato / Wheat / Rice Sample Cards:
    • How it works: Loads an authentic benchmark leaf photo from the local sample_leaf_images/ directory.
    • Why it's not hardcoded: It passes real image bytes into the PyTorch tensor pipeline, running authentic inference and real-time Grad-CAM generation.
  • Choose Photo Button (#leaf-file-input):
    • How it works: Triggers the native HTML5 file selector, allowing the farmer to upload any .jpg or .png photo from phone camera or PC. A JavaScript FileReader renders an instant local thumbnail preview.
  • Farmer's Observation Textarea (#diag-desc):
    • How it works: Allows farmers to enter field observations (e.g., "white powder under leaf"). Passed into the multimodal context pipeline.
  • Run Deep Learning CNN Diagnostic Button (#btn-diagnose):
    • How it works: Posts image and crop metadata to /api/v1/diagnose. Shows an animated spinner (#diag-loading), runs tensor normalization, executes the forward pass, queries disease_db.json, and reveals the diagnosis card (#diag-results) in ~38 ms.
  • 🔥 Grad-CAM Heatmap Toggle Button (#btn-toggle-cam):
    • How it works: Features a tactile clicking animation (active:scale-95). When clicked, it replaces the raw leaf photo with the convolutional attention heatmap base64 data URL and highlights the button in amber (bg-amber-500) with text Show Original Leaf. Clicking again restores the original photo.
  • Economic Prognosis & Disease Spread Simulator Slider (#slider-days):
    • How it works: A range slider from Day 1 to Day 14. Dragging it calls updateEconomicLoss(days), which dynamically re-computes:
      • Canopy Folia Spread: ≈ 12% + (days × 5.8%)
      • Yield Loss: ≈ 5% + (days × 4.6%)
      • Financial Loss / Acre: Calculated against ₹700 per 1% loss (e.g., Day 7 = ₹24,500/acre).
      • Treatment ROI: Compares fungicide cost (₹350) against prevented loss (₹21,000+), demonstrating a 60× ROI.
  • Share WhatsApp Button:
    • How it works: Compiles crop name, severity, day-specific economic risk, organic protocol, and chemical fungicide dosage into a formatted WhatsApp text payload and triggers https://api.whatsapp.com/send?text=....
  • Speaker Icon Button (speakDiagnosis()):
    • How it works: Calls the browser's native SpeechSynthesisUtterance to read aloud the diagnosis and organic remedy in regional phonetics.

PAGE 2: 🛰️ Satellite & Soil Health Dashboard

1. Purpose:
Provides macro-level remote sensing intelligence for smallholder parcels, evaluating seasonal canopy vigor, soil biology balance, and regenerative carbon capture without requiring physical soil lab visits.

2. Technologies Used:

  • Chart.js: Renders dynamic dual-axis line charts for NDVI and EVI vegetation trends.
  • Harmonized Sentinel-2 Multi-Spectral Indices:
    • NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index): NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red) — Measures chlorophyll absorption and photosynthetic active biomass (values 0.2 to 0.85).
    • EVI (Enhanced Vegetation Index): EVI = 2.5 × (NIR - Red) / (NIR + 6 × Red - 7.5 × Blue + 1) — Corrects for atmospheric aerosols and soil background canopy reflectance.
  • ICAR & Bhuvan Soil Taxonomy: Soil metrics modeled for the Upper Gangetic Alluvial Plain.

3. Every Component & Card Explained:

  • Farmer & Parcel Profile Card: Displays unique UFID (Unified Farmer ID), parcel area (2.5 ha), alluvial loam soil classification, and irrigation mode (Tube well + Canal).
  • Soil Health Index Gauge (72.5 / 100): Shows a circular progress metric scoring soil biological health based on pH (6.8 neutral), active nitrogen, phosphorus, and high organic carbon (0.82%).
  • Carbon Sequestration Card (1,850 kg CO₂e/ha/yr): Quantifies the greenhouse gas tonnage sequestered in the soil via regenerative cover crops (Sesbania/Dhaincha) and minimum tillage.
  • Interactive 12-Month NDVI / EVI Historical Curve: A line graph plotting canopy vigor from September 2025 to August 2026. Hovering over data points shows exact vegetative vigor coefficients.
  • Regenerative Crop Rotation Calendar: Maps out sustainable 3-season crop cycles: Kharif (Paddy Basmati), Rabi (Mustard cover crop), and Zaid (Green Moong legume fixing 45 kg/ha atmospheric nitrogen).
  • Micro-Climate Risk Flags: Live warning badges indicating Aphid/Blight outbreak risk (HIGH), water stress index (22%), and frost risk (LOW).

🔬 PART 3: The Deep Learning Architecture & Training Math

1. How MobileNetV3 Was Trained:

  • Dataset: The PlantVillage benchmark dataset containing 54,303 images across 38 classes.
  • Base Network: Pretrained weights from ImageNet-1K were used as the feature extractor.
  • Custom Classification Head:
model.classifier = nn.Sequential( nn.Linear(960, 1024), nn.Hardswish(inplace=True), nn.Dropout(p=0.2), nn.Linear(1024, 38) # 38 plant disease classes )

Why MobileNetV3-Small?

  • Standard convolutions perform spatial and channel filtering simultaneously (computation cost: DK × DK × M × N × DF × DF).
  • MobileNetV3 uses Depthwise Separable Convolutions:
    • Depthwise Convolution: A single convolutional filter per input channel (DK × DK × M × DF × DF).
    • Pointwise Convolution: A 1 × 1 convolution combining channel outputs (M × N × DF × DF).
  • Result: ~85% reduction in computational multiplication steps, enabling 38 ms CPU inference while keeping the weights file at just 16.4 MB.

2. How Grad-CAM Attention Heatmap Works Mathematically:

When your teacher asks: "How do you calculate the heatmap?", give them these 3 steps:

  • Feature Extraction: During the forward pass, we extract feature map Ak from the final layer model.features[-1], shape (1, 960, 7, 7).
  • Gradients via Autograd: We run backpropagation from the winning class score yc:
    αkc = (1 / 49) × ∑i ∑j ( ∂yc / ∂Ai,jk )
    (This gives the importance weight αkc of each of the 960 feature channels).
  • Linear Combination & ReLU:
    LGrad-CAMc = ReLU( ∑k=1..960 αkc Ak )
    The ReLU is critical because we only care about features that increase the likelihood of the disease, ignoring features that decrease it.
  • Upsampling & Jet Colormap: Bilinearly interpolated to (224 × 224), blended at 50% opacity with the original leaf, and encoded as a base64 JPEG string.
